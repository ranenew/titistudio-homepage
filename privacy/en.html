04Data Retention & Statutory Preservation

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the intended purposes. Once the purpose is fulfilled or upon account deletion, data is promptly and irreversibly destroyed. However, minimum statutory transaction evidence required by applicable commercial and consumer protection laws is stored in a strictly segregated vault and permanently deleted upon expiration of the statutory period.

Destruction Principle for User Financial Data:
All private financial, household, budget, investment, loan, insurance, and receipt images entered by the user are NOT statutory transaction records under the E-Commerce Act. Therefore, upon account deletion, all user-entered financial and asset data is immediately and permanently destroyed without residual retention.

09Account Deletion & Data Destruction

When you request account deletion (withdrawal), we promptly and permanently destroy your personal data in accordance with the following principles:

1. Request Channels & Authentication

  • In-App Self-Service: Open the Money Synapse app > Settings > Delete Account. Deletion is authenticated with your active session.
  • Web Deletion Portal: If the app is uninstalled, you can authenticate directly on our official Account Deletion Page using your existing login method (Email/Password or Social Login).
  • Email Guidance: Our support email (`titi@titistudio.kr`) provides procedural assistance only. To prevent identity theft, account deletion cannot be executed purely on an unauthenticated email request.

2. Execution Sequence & Purge Process

  • Step 1 (Write Fence): Upon receiving the withdrawal request, new financial writes to the account are immediately locked.
  • Step 2 (Storage Destruction): User-uploaded files (receipt photos, goal images, wishlist photos) across cloud storage buckets are verified and completely removed.
  • Step 3 (Financial & Account CASCADE Purge): The user's authentication account (`auth.users`) is permanently deleted, triggering a database-level cascading deletion (CASCADE) that completely destroys all accounts, cards, transactions, budgets, savings, investments, loans, insurance, and memos.
  • Step 4 (Statutory Record Segregation): Only minimum statutory transaction evidence required by the E-Commerce Act (such as subscription payments and credit purchase ledgers) is transferred to segregated storage stripped of all raw personal and financial content. (Dedicated vault architecture is coupled with the V2 engine deployment.)

3. Disposal Methods & Backup Policy

  • Electronic Records: Permanently erased using technical methods that render recovery impossible.
  • System Backups: Disaster-recovery snapshots are isolated and naturally overwritten and purged in accordance with standard backup rotation cycles (individual restores from backup are disabled).
  • Statutory Purge: Segregated records in the legal vault are automatically and permanently purged once their respective 5-year or 3-year statutory periods expire.

Store Subscription Cancellation Notice:
Deleting your account does not automatically cancel recurring in-app subscriptions managed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To avoid unwanted renewal charges, please cancel recurring subscriptions directly in your platform account settings.

10Free Trial (7-Day Pro) Data Processing

Money Synapse may provide new users with a complimentary 7-day Pro trial. In connection with this promotion, the following information is processed:

  • Data Items: Timestamps of trial issuance, activation, and expiration; pseudonymized abuse-prevention digests (one-way cryptographic hash)
  • Purpose: Enforcing the one-trial-per-person policy and preventing fraudulent repeated re-registration exploiting free benefits
  • Legal Basis: Article 15(1)(6) of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (Legitimate interests)
  • Retention Period: Preserved in an abuse-prevention ledger for 1 year from issuance, after which it is irreversibly purged. Plaintext emails, names, or phone numbers are never retained for this purpose.
  • User Recourse: Users may challenge trial eligibility determinations by contacting customer support.

13Security Safeguards

In accordance with Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act, we implement rigorous technical and administrative safeguards:

  • Encryption: Full TLS/HTTPS protocol for transit and one-way bcrypt hashing with salted credentials for passwords.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Database-enforced isolation ensuring users cannot access or view other users' records.
  • Segregated Retention: Statutory evidence for withdrawn users is stored in an isolated database schema inaccessible to regular API clients.
  • Access Control: Minimal administrative privileges and execution logging for all core backend components.
